Positional / RoPE improvementsDaRoPE (Data-aware RoPE) — arXiv 2609.34556 (28 Sep 2026). Keeps standard RoPE on fast frequency bands but replaces slow bands with bounded, data-dependent coordinates learned from context. Reduces recency bias and improves non-text + language modeling / extrapolation. Directly addresses a known RoPE weakness.
  Paper: "RoPE is Dead, Long Live RoPE: Towards Scalable Data-aware Positional Encodings" (Lévy, Videau, Yehya, King, d'Ascoli, Moreau) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34556

MoE variants suited to small modelsFoil / Looped MoE — arXiv 2609.35751 (28 Sep). “Flatten the experts, untie the attention.” Makes looped MoE work better by increasing experts-per-layer + number of passes while sharing experts/routers. Better expert utilization at fixed active compute/params. Relevant if you want parameter efficiency without huge active FLOPs.
  Paper: "How to Loop MoE: Flatten the Experts, Untie the Attention" (Wang et al.) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35751
MoSE (Mixture of Slimmable Experts) — ICML 2026. Each expert is itself width-slimmable, so you can trade accuracy vs. compute more continuously at inference.
  Paper: "MoSE: Mixture of Slimmable Experts for Efficient and Adaptive Language Models" (Tastan, Laskaridis, Nandakumar, Horváth) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06154
  (Not the unrelated embodied-robotics "MoSE", arXiv 2507.07818.)
Extreme sparsity examples: Sigma-MoE-Tiny style (very high total/active ratio, fine-grained experts, only 1 expert active).
  Paper: "Sigma-MoE-Tiny Technical Report" (Microsoft) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.16248 (~20B total / ~0.5B active, 96 experts per layer, top-1)
Other MoE notes: head-wise routing (MH-MoE) to reduce catastrophic forgetting; ZipMoE for efficient on-device serving; scaling-law papers clarifying active vs. total parameters.
  MH-MoE: "Multi-Head Attention as a Source of Catastrophic Forgetting in MoE Transformers" (ICML 2026) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12587

Other architectural tricksLooped / recurrent transformers — Strong X discussion (e.g., Aleksa Gordić, mid-Sep). Reuse a block of layers multiple times (prelude → recurring core looped r times → coda). Adds a new scaling axis (recurrent depth / latent compute) without growing parameters or verbal CoT length. Training samples variable r; supports early exit, KV reuse, and speculative decoding. Complements MoE nicely.
  No single paper (X thread not found). Relevant papers:

Self-Pruning Transformer / Universal Attention — Extreme KV-cache compression while keeping RoPE + softmax expressivity. Achieves large compression ratios with better downstream performance.
  Paper: "A Self-Pruning Transformer: Extreme KV Cache Compression with Universal Attention" (Phil. Trans. R. Soc. A; no arXiv ID found) — https://research.ibm.com/publications/a-self-pruning-transformer-extreme-kv-cache-compression-with-universal-attention

  Cross-head sparse attention: "Less Is More: Training-Free Sparse Attention with Global Locality for Efficient Reasoning" / ICML 2026 title "Less Is More: Fast and Accurate Reasoning with Cross-Head Unified Sparse Attention" — https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.07101
